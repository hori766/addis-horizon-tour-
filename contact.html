Common
questions.

Everything visitors typically want to know before booking an Addis Ababa city tour. Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp — we reply within minutes.

What forms of payment do you accept? +

We accept payment in USD cash, Ethiopian Birr, and major credit cards. Payment details and booking confirmation will be arranged when you reach out via WhatsApp or email.

Do you offer airport pickup from Bole International? +

Yes. Hotel and airport pickup/drop-off are included in every tour package — Half-Day, Full-Day, and Night Life. We meet you at Bole International Airport (ADD) or directly at your hotel.

Can I take a tour during my Ethiopian Airlines layover? +

Absolutely — layover tours are one of our specialties. No transit visa is required for Ethiopian Airlines passengers with an 8+ hour stopover. Our Half-Day Tour (5–6 hours) is designed specifically for layovers, with timing arranged around your flight schedule.

What's included in the tour price? +

Every tour includes: a licensed English-speaking guide, a private vehicle with driver, all entrance fees to museums and sites, a traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony, hotel or airport pickup and drop-off, bottled water, and free WiFi in the vehicle.

How do I book a tour? +

The fastest way is WhatsApp at 0907089099 — available 24/7 with most replies in under a minute. You can also email ADDISHORIZONCITYTOUR@GMAIL.COM or use the contact form. We respond within minutes.

Are the tours private or group tours? +

100% private. You'll never share a vehicle with strangers. Your guide, driver, vehicle, and itinerary are exclusively yours — paced however you want.

What language do your guides speak? +

All licensed guides speak fluent English and Amharic. Additional languages (French, German, Italian, Spanish) may be available on request — just let us know when you book.

How much does an Addis Ababa city tour cost? +

Our Half-Day City Tour is $90 per person, the Full-Day City Tour is $120 per person, and the Night Life Tour is $100 per person. All prices are fully inclusive — guide, vehicle, entrance fees, coffee ceremony, and hotel/airport transfers.
